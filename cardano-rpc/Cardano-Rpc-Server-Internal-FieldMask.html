Cardano.Rpc.Server.Internal.FieldMask

Description

Normal form of google.protobuf.FieldMask paths, ported from addFieldPath and toFieldMask of protobuf Java's FieldMaskTree.

Paths start at the response message. A path may continue through repeated message fields and then applies to each element. Paths that do not resolve are dropped whole. An absent or empty mask, or a * path, selects everything. A mask whose paths all drop selects nothing.

data FieldFill (m :: Type -> Type) msg Source #

Effects that fill the fields a mask selects; Nothing when it selects none of them. Effects run left to right.

The type, from the outside in:

  • FieldMaskTree msg ->: each fill sees the part of the mask that applies to msg.
  • Maybe: Nothing when the mask selects none of the fill's fields, so runFill never calls its runner (no node session) for an empty selection.
  • Ap m: the effect that obtains the selected values. Ap gives m (Endo msg) the Semigroup that the Monoid of Maybe needs, and runs combined effects left to right.
  • Endo msg: the setters for the selected fields; (<>) composes them and runFill applies them to defMessage.
